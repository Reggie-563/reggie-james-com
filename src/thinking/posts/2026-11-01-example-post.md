This is an example article. It stays hidden on the live site because it is marked as a draft. Use it as a reference for how headings, lists and quotes look.

## A section heading

Body text sits at a comfortable reading width with generous line spacing. Links look like [this one](/thinking/).

- A point in a list
- Another point, a little longer, to show how list items wrap onto a second line when they need to
- A third point

> A pull quote stands out from the body text for the one line you want people to remember.

### A smaller heading

Short paragraphs read best on screen. Two or three sentences each is plenty.
